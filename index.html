Browse all datasets

Discover open cultural datasets from institutions across Switzerland, all in one place. While the datasets are hosted on various platforms, including opendata.swiss, GBIF, Wikimedia Commons, and Zenodo, this catalogue brings them together in a single, easy-to-browse collection.
